## Cross-linking Rules

- Every article should link to at least one relevant `/services/*` page and one of `/projects`, `/process`, or `/about`.
- Every article ends with a distinct call to action pointing to `/contact` — wording should vary between articles rather than repeating a template.
- Related articles within the same cluster should cross-link where it's genuinely useful to the reader, not on every post automatically.
- Do not add an article to this map until it meets the standards in `docs/website-standards.md` (no placeholder content, no SEO-factory language, real engineering substance).
